museauto

museauto is a personal automation tool that uploads videos to its owner's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API. It runs on the owner's own computer, is used by a single person for their own channel, and is not offered to the public.

What it does: upload a finished video file, set its title, description, tags, thumbnail and subtitles, and check the upload status.
