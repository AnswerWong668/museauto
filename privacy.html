Privacy Policy — museauto

Effective date: 15 November 2026.

What this app is

museauto is a personal tool used by one person to upload videos to their own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the app accesses

When the owner authorizes the app with their Google account, the app can access their own YouTube channel only, for the following purposes:

  • Uploading video files and setting each video's title, description, tags, thumbnail and subtitles;
  • Reading upload status to confirm a video was published.

The app does not access any other Google service or any other person's data.

How data is stored

The Google OAuth tokens that authorize these actions are stored only on the owner's own computer. The app has no server: no personal data is transmitted to, stored on, or processed by any server operated by the app.

What data is collected or shared

The app collects no personal data, runs no analytics, shows no advertising, and shares no data with any third party. The only third party involved is Google, whose YouTube Data API receives the video file and metadata the owner chooses to upload, as required to perform the upload itself. No data is sold.

Data retention and deletion

Because the app stores nothing on a server, there is no server-side data to retain or delete. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from their Google account settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions), which immediately stops all access.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
